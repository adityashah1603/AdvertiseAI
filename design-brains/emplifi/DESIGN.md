## Applying it

Emplifi is a customer-experience platform for social, care and commerce.
Tone is crisp and metric-led. Assets sit on a deep navy ground, with
periwinkle geometric shapes - circles, arcs, a thin connecting line - used
to frame a photo or a product surface in the lower half. Prefer product
surfaces and real UI over abstract illustration. Generous whitespace, high
contrast.

The orange accent is reserved for the call to action and is never used as a
fill. A CTA is an orange label inside a 2px orange outline on the navy
ground. Periwinkle may be used as a fill; orange may not.
